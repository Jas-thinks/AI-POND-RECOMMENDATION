GIS Methodology Reference

CS559 Theoretical Foundation
1. Digital Elevation Model (DEM) Grid

A matrix of cells containing terrain height values. It describes topography. We fetch this dynamically via Open-Meteo API or generate a synthetic bowl basin for offline tests.

2. Slope (Horn's Central Difference)

Measures steepness. Flat slope limits erosion and facilitates stable embankment construction. Computed as the rate of change of elevation in orthogonal directions.

3. D8 Flow Routing Algorithm

Water flows from each cell to the steepest downslope neighboring cell among the 8 surrounding cells. Sinks are cells with no lower neighbors where natural ponding occurs.

4. Flow Accumulation & Catchment Delineation

Flow Accumulation is the count of cells draining through a cell. Delineation traces the upstream network in reverse using Breadth-First Search (BFS) to map the total runoff collection basin.
